## 5. Anti-Scam Guardrails & Quality Assurance

1. **Zero Fake Upsells**: Clearly disclose the exact state filing fee separately from agency service fees.
2. **Transparent Annual Costs**: Prevent predatory renewal charges by publishing exact state annual fees (e.g., NM = $0/year, WY = $60/year).
3. **IRS CP-575 Authenticity**: Inform founders that the IRS issues CP 575 or 147C letters directly; never accept generic third-party certificates.
4. **Permanent Registered Agent**: Provide 1 full year included, with flat renewals capped under $99/year.
